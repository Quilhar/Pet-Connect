Our Mission

 PetConnect is a passion project built around a simple idea: finding the right pet should feel personal, hopeful, and accessible. I created it to help people discover adoptable animals near them and find the information they need to take the next step toward adoption. Every search is a chance to help an animal find a loving home and help a person find a companion.

-David Tit
